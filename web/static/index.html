Add to your home screen

iPhone: open this page in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

Android: open this page in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen or Install app. After that, you can use Share in the Pinterest, Instagram or X app and pick Stash.

Where do my files go?

Save puts the file in your downloads (the Files app on iPhone).

Photos opens your phone's share menu. On iPhone, choose Save Image or Save Video to put it in Photos.

Only public posts work. Private accounts and posts that need a login can't be saved.
